# Your account

Everything here is under the person icon at the top right.

## Profile

- **Display name** — what other people see next to your edits.
- **Email** — optional. Compendio does not send email; this is a directory entry, not a delivery
  address.
- **Change password** — you need your current password to set a new one.

There is no self-service password reset, because there is no email. If you are locked out, an
administrator sets a new password for you.

## Language

The language menu at the top switches the interface between the languages this instance offers.
Your choice is remembered against your account, so it follows you to another browser.

This is the *interface* language — buttons, menus and messages. It does not translate the pages
themselves; those are in whatever language their authors wrote them. It does, however, rank pages
in your language higher in search, and decide which version of a translated page you land on.

An administrator can force a single language for the whole instance, in which case the menu does
not appear.

## Theme

Light or dark, from the icon at the top. It follows your system setting until you choose.

## Notifications

The bell shows what concerns you:

| Notification | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **A page you own is due for review** | Its review date has passed |
| **A page you own was edited on disk** | Somebody changed the file directly on the server |
| **The source of a translation you own has changed** | Your translation may now be out of date |
| **Please confirm you have read this** | A page is waiting for your acknowledgment |
| **Overdue: please confirm you have read this** | It has been waiting a while |

**Mark read** clears one; **Mark all read** clears the lot. Notifications live in Compendio — there
is no email, so the bell and the dashboard are where they appear.

## Signing out

Under the person icon. **Stay signed in** on the sign-in screen keeps your session across browser
restarts; leave it off on a shared computer.

# Getting started

Compendio is your organization's wiki. Every page you see here is a Markdown file in a folder on
the server — nothing is locked inside a database you cannot open.

## What is on the screen

| Where | What it is |
|---|---|
| Left | The **page tree** — your folders and pages. On a phone it hides behind the menu button. |
| Top | The **search box**, which searches inside pages, not just their titles. |
| Top right | Theme, language, notifications, your profile, and this guide. |
| Middle | Whatever you are reading or writing. |

## The dashboard

Signing in lands you on the dashboard, not on a blank page. It answers three questions:

- **Your pages due for review** — pages you own that are past their review date.
- **Recent notifications** — what happened that concerns you.
- **Pages to confirm you have read** — anything waiting for your acknowledgment.

If all three are empty, there is nothing for you to do, which is the point.

## Finding your way around

- Click a folder in the tree to expand it; click a page to read it.
- Press **Ctrl-K** (**Cmd-K** on a Mac) anywhere to jump to a page by name.
- Type in the search box to search inside every page you are allowed to read.

## What you can do depends on your role

Everybody has one of three roles, set by an administrator:

| Role | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Reader** | Can read. Cannot change anything, ever. |
| **Editor** | Can read and write, where a folder allows it. |
| **Administrator** | Everything, including the Administration screens. |

Your role is a ceiling, not a grant. A Reader can never write, whatever an individual folder's
rules say. Within your role, access is decided folder by folder — so an Editor may be able to write
in *IT* and only read in *HR*.

If a page seems to be missing, it may simply not be visible to you. Compendio never tells you that
a page exists but is off limits, because that would itself leak information.
